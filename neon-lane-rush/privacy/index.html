Neon Lane Rush: Overdrive

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 September 2026

This policy describes how Neon Lane Rush: Overdrive handles information when you use the Android game. The app is published by Ajdar Ullah.

1. Information handled by the app

The game stores gameplay progress locally on your device, such as career progress, unlocked content, settings, missions, statistics and purchase entitlement state. This local game data is used to operate the game and is not intended to identify you personally.

The app also integrates third-party Google services for advertising, consent management and in-app purchases. Those services may process information as described below.

2. Advertising and consent

Neon Lane Rush: Overdrive uses Google Mobile Ads to provide optional rewarded advertisements and limited interstitial advertisements. Google's advertising services may process information such as device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, advertising interactions, diagnostics and other data needed to deliver, measure and protect advertising services.

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage privacy/advertising consent choices before advertising is initialized. You may also be offered privacy options that allow you to review or change applicable consent choices.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use Google services, see Google's privacy documentation and policies.

3. In-app purchases

The game uses Google Play Billing for optional purchases such as Remove Ads and a Starter Pack. Payment information is processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive your full payment-card information. The app may store purchase entitlement state locally so purchased features can be recognized.

4. Data sharing

The developer does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Google service providers when you use advertising, consent-management or Google Play purchase functionality. Their processing is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Children's privacy

The game is not designed specifically for children under 13. The intended Google Play target audience is teenagers and adults. If the target-audience configuration changes in the future, this policy and the app's compliance configuration will be updated as required.

6. Data retention and deletion

Gameplay progression and settings are primarily stored locally on your device. Removing the app generally removes this local app data, subject to Android/Google Play platform behavior. Information processed by Google services is retained according to Google's applicable retention policies.

The game does not currently provide a developer-operated user account system or developer-operated cloud database containing player profiles.

7. Security

The app uses standard Android and Google platform services and is configured to avoid cleartext network traffic. No method of electronic processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but reasonable technical measures are used in the application architecture.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game, its third-party services or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
