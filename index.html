Research focus

Signals into insight.

My work centers on extracting robust, clinically and practically useful information from noisy biomedical measurements and wearable sensors.

Biomedical DSP

Physiological signal analysis

Methods for extracting informative features and patterns from signals such as ECG, EMG and related physiological data.

Machine Learning

Learning from biosignals

Feature engineering, dimensionality reduction, classification and validation workflows designed for biomedical datasets.

Wearable Sensing

Mobile & wearable systems

Signal-processing approaches for sensor-rich mobile and wearable devices, with an emphasis on practical deployment constraints.
